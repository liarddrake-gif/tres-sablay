Game Description

TRES, SABLAY! is a Filipino-themed trivia and strategy game inspired by the lively atmosphere of a traditional Filipino peryahan. Players compete in a reverse Tic-Tac-Toe challenge where getting three symbols in a row can lead to a surprising twist.

🧠

Game Concept

The game combines strategy, quick thinking, and Filipino trivia. Players take turns placing their symbols while carefully avoiding three in a row. When a player completes three, the opposing player faces a trivia challenge that can determine the outcome of the game.

🎠

Peryahan ng Talino

The colorful carnival design represents the excitement of Filipino peryahan — complete with bright lights, carnival rides, games, and festive entertainment. Here, knowledge and strategy become the ultimate attractions.

🎯 How To Play

Simple rules. Big surprises.

1️⃣

Take Your Turn

Players take turns placing their X or O on the game board.

2️⃣

Avoid Three!

Be careful! Completing three symbols in a row triggers the game's special trivia challenge.

3️⃣

Face the Trivia

The opposing player answers a Filipino trivia question to determine the final result.
